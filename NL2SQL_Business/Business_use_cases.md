# 📊 Business use cases

## Use Case 1: Wealth Manager Dashboard

**Scenario:** A wealth manager needs quick insights about client portfolios.

**Sample Questions:**

```txt
1. "Show me all clients with investment accounts over $100,000"
2. "Which stocks are most popular among our clients?"
3. "List clients who haven't made any investments in the last 6 months"
4. "Which stock tickers are most heavily held in each account type?"
```

**Benefits:**

- Instant access to client data
- No need to write SQL queries
- Can ask follow-up questions naturally
- Faster client service


## Use Case 2: Risk Analysis

**Scenario:** Risk analyst needs to identify portfolio concentrations.

**Sample Questions:**

```txt
1. "Which clients have more than 50% of their portfolio in a single stock?"
2. "Show me all clients with high-risk credit ratings"
3. "What's the average portfolio diversification?"
4. "List accounts with unusual trading patterns"
```

**Benefits:**

- Quick risk assessment
- Identify concentration risks
- Monitor compliance
- Proactive risk management

## Use Case 3: Customer Service

**Scenario:** Customer service rep needs to answer client questions.

**Sample Questions:**

```txt
1. "What's the current value of account #12345?"
2. "Show me all transactions for John Smith"
3. "Which stocks has this customer invested in?"
4. "What's the performance of their portfolio this year?"
```

**Benefits:**

- Faster customer response times
- Accurate information
- No need for technical skills
- Better customer experience


## Use Case 4: Executive Reporting

**Scenario:** Executive needs high-level business metrics.

**Sample Questions:**

```txt
1. "What's our total assets under management?"
2. "How many active investment accounts do we have?"
3. "What's the average account balance by state?"
4. "Show me growth trends in new accounts"
```

**Benefits:**

- Real-time business metrics
- No waiting for reports
- Ad-hoc analysis capability
- Data-driven decisions
